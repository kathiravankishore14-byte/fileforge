OnlineToolsWeb started from a simple frustration: too many "free" online tools bury the thing you actually need behind a forced sign-up, a nagging upgrade prompt, or a quiet upload of your file to a server you know nothing about. So this site does the opposite. Every tool here — resizing an image, merging a PDF, generating a QR code — runs directly in your browser, on your own device. Your file never has to leave your computer to get the job done.

How it works

Every tool is built with ordinary web technology (JavaScript running in your browser) rather than a server processing your files somewhere else. That's what makes the "no upload" promise on almost every tool here genuinely true, not just a marketing line — the code that resizes your image or merges your PDF is the same code your browser is already running the page with, and it never needs to send your file anywhere to do its job.

What's free, and what might not always be

Every tool's core functionality is free to use, with no account required and no limit on how many times you can use it. As the site grows, some advanced features — batch processing many files at once, saved presets, larger file limits — may become part of an optional paid tier down the line. The tools you rely on today staying free is the whole point of this site, and that's not changing.

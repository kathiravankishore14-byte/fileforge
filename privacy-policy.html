OnlineToolsWeb ("we", "us", "the site") provides free browser-based tools for working with PDFs, images, and documents. This page explains what happens to your files and what we do and don't collect. The short version: every tool runs on your own device, no file you work with is ever sent anywhere, and we collect close to nothing about you either.

How your files are processed

Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser. When you drop in a PDF, image, or document, it's processed using JavaScript running on your own device: it is never transmitted anywhere, we never see it, and it disappears the moment you close or refresh the tab. This includes Remove Background: the AI model that finds the subject in your photo downloads once (and is cached by your browser after that), then runs locally every time you use the tool. Your photo itself never leaves your device.

What we collect

We don't require accounts, logins, or any personal information to use this site. We don't run advertising trackers, and as of this policy's last update, we don't use any analytics or cookie-based tracking. Like effectively every website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) automatically logs standard technical data for every request, things like IP address, browser type, and timestamp, purely for security, abuse prevention, and keeping the site running. We don't access those logs to identify individual visitors, and we don't sell or share them.

Cookies

We don't set any cookies of our own. Cloudflare, as our hosting/CDN provider, may set a small number of essential cookies related to security and performance (for example, to help distinguish real visitors from bots). These aren't used to track you across other sites.

Third parties

The only third party involved in running this site is Cloudflare, our hosting provider, which automatically logs basic technical request data as described above. No file you work with on this site is ever sent to Cloudflare, us, or anyone else.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect any information from them.
